# Companion article: contents and scope

Tatsuhiko Takimoto, *Global identifiability of a behavioral SIR model for every fixed real saturation exponent k ≥ 2*, article 3.0 (27 October 2026), publication display edition dated 10 November 2026.

This directory contains the article [TeX](paper-behavioral-sir-v3.0-en.tex) and [PDF](paper-behavioral-sir-v3.0-en.pdf), together with this contents guide. The article supplies the rational-cover argument cited by the minimal-jet paper. The needed three-exponent proof is also given in Lemma 3 and Appendix B of the [main article](../paper-sir-minimal-jets-v1-en.pdf).

## Scope of this extract

The companion article's Section 9 (Exact finite checks) describes computations from its original supplement: finite-field observation matrices and nonzero minors, rational and irrational identity checks, and a missing-factor negative control over Q(sqrt(7)). Those data, programs and the companion's original verification record are not included in this article-only extract. Running the minimal-jet packet does not reproduce those companion computations. No version-fixed public URL or DOI for that original supplement is supplied here.

The [packet verification record](../VERIFICATION-RECORD.md) documents the minimal-jet paper and this extract's provenance; it is not the companion's full verification record. The [main README](../README.md#reproduce-offline) describes the computations actually included: the three minimal-jet algebraic certificates, seven interval certificates, generated tables and the Appendix B identity checks.
